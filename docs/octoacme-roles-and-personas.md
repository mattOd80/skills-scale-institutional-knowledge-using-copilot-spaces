# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises. This expanded set includes core product and delivery roles, as well as specialized roles in user experience, operations, quality assurance, support, and security to support cross-functional collaboration and institutional knowledge sharing.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX Designer

### Role Summary
UX Designers design workflows, user interfaces, and interaction patterns that deliver intuitive and accessible user experiences. They partner with Product Managers and Developers throughout the feature development lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Design user workflows, wireframes, and UI components
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Ensure accessibility standards and inclusive design principles
- Partner with Developers and Product Managers on feature refinement
- Maintain design systems and component libraries

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible user experiences
- Ensure user-centric decisions guide product development
- Reduce user friction and improve satisfaction metrics

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and critiques
- User testing sessions and research findings
- Feature refinement and prototyping discussions

---

## DevOps Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps Engineers maintain CI/CD pipelines, monitoring, and infrastructure automation. They handle release engineering, deployment processes, and ensure reliable, automated delivery of software to production.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain CI/CD pipelines and deployment automation
- Monitor production systems and handle incident response
- Manage infrastructure as code and cloud resources
- Collaborate with Developers and QA to automate quality gates
- Handle release engineering, deployment, and rollback procedures

### Goals
- Ensure reliable, automated deployments with minimal downtime
- Improve observability and system reliability
- Reduce manual toil through automation

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and deployment coordination
- Incident response and post-mortems
- Pipeline updates and infrastructure changes

---

## Support Specialist

### Role Summary
Support Specialists interact directly with end-users, capturing feedback, bug reports, and feature requests. They serve as the voice of the customer and close the feedback loop between users and the product team.

### Responsibilities
- Handle user inquiries and troubleshoot issues
- Document and escalate bug reports to development teams
- Capture user feedback and feature requests
- Provide contextual information for root cause analysis
- Communicate escalation paths to Product and Project Managers

### Goals
- Close the feedback loop and improve customer satisfaction
- Reduce time to resolution for user issues
- Ensure customer insights inform product decisions

### Typical Communication
- Bug reports and issue escalations
- User feedback summaries and trends
- Support metrics and resolution updates

---

## Security Lead

### Role Summary
Security Leads review products for security vulnerabilities, coordinate incident response, and ensure the product maintains security and compliance standards. They work cross-functionally to embed security throughout the development lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct security reviews and threat modeling
- Coordinate security incident response with stakeholders
- Define security requirements and compliance standards
- Work with DevOps and Developers for secure deployment practices
- Track and remediate security vulnerabilities

### Goals
- Maintain product security and compliance
- Minimize security incidents and exposure
- Embed security best practices across teams

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews and assessments
- Incident coordination and response
- Threat assessments and vulnerability reports

---

## QA Automation Engineer

### Role Summary
QA Automation Engineers develop and maintain test automation frameworks and scripts. They work with manual QA testers and Developers to establish quality gates and ensure comprehensive test coverage.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain test automation frameworks and scripts
- Develop automated tests for functional, integration, and regression testing
- Work with Developers to establish quality gates in CI/CD
- Report test results, coverage metrics, and quality trends
- Collaborate with manual QA on test strategy and coverage

### Goals
- Improve release quality through comprehensive automation
- Reduce manual testing effort and cycle time
- Increase test coverage and confidence in releases

### Typical Communication
- Test reports and quality metrics
- Automation strategy and framework updates
- Quality gate definitions and coverage reviews

---

## Cross-Role Collaboration

### How New Personas Interact with Existing Roles

**UX Designer interactions:**
- **Product Managers**: Collaborate on feature definition, validate solutions through user research, align on user experience goals
- **Developers**: Partner on implementation feasibility, component design, and accessibility requirements
- **Project Managers**: Provide design estimates, participate in planning, flag UX risks

**DevOps Engineer interactions:**
- **Developers**: Collaborate on deployment automation, infrastructure needs, and production debugging
- **QA Automation Engineer**: Work together to integrate automated tests into CI/CD pipelines
- **Project Managers**: Coordinate release schedules, communicate deployment risks and timelines

**Support Specialist interactions:**
- **Product Managers**: Share user feedback, feature requests, and pain points to inform roadmap
- **Developers**: Escalate bugs with contextual information for faster resolution
- **Project Managers**: Report on support metrics, user satisfaction trends, and escalation patterns

**Security Lead interactions:**
- **Developers**: Review code for vulnerabilities, advise on secure coding practices
- **DevOps Engineer**: Collaborate on secure deployment, infrastructure hardening, and compliance
- **Product Managers**: Define security requirements, balance security with user experience

**QA Automation Engineer interactions:**
- **Developers**: Establish quality gates, review testability of code, share test results
- **DevOps Engineer**: Integrate automated tests into deployment pipelines
- **Project Managers**: Report quality metrics, testing progress, and release readiness

These expanded personas enable clearer accountability, reduce role confusion, and ensure that critical perspectives—user experience, operations, security, quality, and customer support—are formally represented in project planning and execution.
